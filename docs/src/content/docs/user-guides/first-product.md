A product record is the top of a teardown. Everything else, the components, materials and
photos, hangs below it.

## What you need

- A verified account. See [Getting started](../getting-started/).
- The product, still in one piece.
- A phone or camera.

## Steps

1. Sign in at [app.r9lab.io](https://app.r9lab.io) and open **Products**.
1. Choose the button that adds a new product. The form opens.
1. Add a photo of the intact product, as it arrived. Do this before you open it.
1. Type the **Name**. It is the only required field. Use what is on the label, such as
   "Cordless drill".
1. Under **Product type**, type a word such as "drill" and choose the closest match. If no
   word finds it, choose **Browse categories**. If none fits, leave it empty.
1. Choose **Create product**. To start another record straight away, choose
   **Create & add another**.

![The new product form with an Add photos area, a Name field filled with "Cordless drill", and the Product type search field.](/images/guides/new-product-form.webp)

After you create it, the record opens. Add the brand, model, measurements and notes there.

![A product record for a cordless drill. It shows the photo, the name, the type, brand and model, the weight and the size, and a menu with Overview, Components and Properties.](/images/guides/product-overview.webp)

*Example record with illustrative photos.*

## What good data looks like

- The name says what the product is, not what you did with it.
- Brand and model match the label. Leave them empty if the product has none.
- The first photo shows the whole product, wear and damage included.
- Anything you are unsure about is marked as unsure, not guessed.
