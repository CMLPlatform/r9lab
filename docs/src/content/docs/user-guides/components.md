title: Add components

A component is a part you take out of the product. Components can have their own components, so
the record follows the physical breakdown.

## What you need

- A product record. See [Add your first product](../first-product/).
- The product, taken apart one step at a time.

## Steps

1. Open the product, or the component the part came out of.
1. In **Components**, choose **Add component**.
1. Photograph the part before you take it out, where this is safe.
1. Type the **Name**, such as "Battery pack".
1. Choose a component type or material, if one fits.
1. Set **How many of these** when the same part appears more than once.
1. Choose **Create component**. Use **Create & add another** to go on with the next part.

![The new component form. It says Component of: Cordless drill, with a Name field filled with "Battery pack", a type button, a count of 1 and Create component buttons.](/images/guides/new-component-form.webp)

The product then lists its components, with a photo for each one that has one.

![The Components list of a cordless drill with three parts: a battery pack, a motor assembly and a housing. The housing has no photo yet.](/images/guides/product-components.webp)

## When to make a component

Make a component when:

- the part has a clear function
- the part has its own material or circularity relevance
- the part needs its own photos or notes
- the part may be useful to compare across products

:::tip[A useful rule of thumb]
If you would photograph it separately and write notes about it, it deserves its own record. A
battery pack is a component. A single screw usually is not.
:::

## What good data looks like

- Each component sits under the part it came out of.
- The photos show the part in place first, then on its own.
- Parts you weighed have a weight. Parts you did not weigh have none, not `0`.
- Notes say how hard the part was to remove and what you had to break.
